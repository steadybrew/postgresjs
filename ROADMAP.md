# Roadmap

The roadmap is the [Roadmap project](https://github.com/orgs/steadybrew/projects/1). Every planned fix and feature is an issue there, and its Status says where it stands: In progress, Next, Later or Shipped.

- Labels give the area (`area: protocol`, `area: connections`, `area: serialization` and so on) and the kind (`bug`, `enhancement`, `documentation`).
- Issues labelled [`upstream`](https://github.com/steadybrew/postgresjs/issues?q=is%3Aopen+label%3Aupstream) answer reports on [porsager/postgres](https://github.com/porsager/postgres/issues) and link to them.
- Issues labelled `unverified` have no reproduction yet.

Comments and reactions on the issues help decide the order. Released changes are in the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md), and the upstream reports they fix are listed in the [README](README.md#fixed-postgresjs-issues).
